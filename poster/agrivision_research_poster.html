Problem & Abstract

Agricultural Crisis: Plant diseases cause 20% to 40% global crop yield losses annually ($220B+ economic loss). Farmers face diagnosis delays of 4-10 days, leading to pesticide overuse and financial debt.

AgriVision Solution: An integrated full-stack AI ecosystem delivering sub-5-second disease detection, 3-tier remedial action plans (Organic, Biological, Chemical), and real-time mandi pricing intelligence to eliminate middleman exploitation.

03

Deep Learning Pipeline

Transfer Learning Architecture: Utilizes MobileNetV2 with depthwise separable convolutions for lightweight, edge-compatible edge computing.

1
Preprocessing: 224x224 RGB resizing, Z-score normalization, random flips & rotation augmentation.
2
Feature Extraction: Inverted Residual Blocks with Linear Bottlenecks to preserve manifold info.
3
Classification Head: GlobalAvgPool2D → Dense(256, ReLU) → Dropout(0.4) → Softmax(38).
4
Confidence Gate: τ = 0.10 anomaly threshold to accurately isolate healthy foliage.

About AgriVision

AgriVision is an intelligent disease detection system designed to detect diseases in plants, leaves, and fruits using advanced image-based analysis. It leverages the power of Computer Vision technologies to provide fast and accurate disease identification from uploaded images — supporting farmers, researchers, and agriculture professionals with instant.

🌿

38 Disease Classes

Covers diseases across Apple, Tomato, Grape, Corn, Potato, and many more crops — enabling broad agricultural utility.

⚡

Real-Time Detection

Flask-powered backend delivers disease predictions and care recommendations in under one second.

📊

95%+ Accuracy

Powered by the advanced Plant.id AI classification engine, trained on millions of global botanical specimens.

🌾 Our Agronomic Purpose

Empowering Agriculture Through Intelligent Vision

Bridging deep machine learning with grassroots agronomy to protect crop yields, support rural growers, and build climate-resilient farming futures.

🌱 Mission & Innovation

Cultivating Sustainable Agricultural Futures

Designed to empower smallholders and agricultural researchers with instant botanical AI diagnostics, fostering sustainable cultivation, reducing disease spread, and curbing chemical runoff.

🏡 Rural Community Impact

From Academic Research to Field Impact

Connecting academic computer vision models directly to agrarian communities, providing accessible disease detection that safeguards family farms and local food systems.

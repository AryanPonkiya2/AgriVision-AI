High-precision AI detection begins with clear leaf captures. Follow our field scouting recommendations for reliable pathology results.

📸 Field Scouting Protocol

Precision In-Field Leaf Scouting

Capture affected leaves under natural diffuse daylight. Keep the camera steady and focus squarely on the lesion boundary to help the neural network identify microscopic fungal spores and spot patterns.

🛡️ Early Intervention

Early Pathogen Intervention

Scanning crops at the earliest sign of leaf discoloration, rust pustules, or powdery mildew preserves vegetative health and prevents severe defoliation during critical flowering and grain-filling stages.
